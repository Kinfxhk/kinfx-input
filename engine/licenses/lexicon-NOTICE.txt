## lexicon/hk — Cantonese words and 4-character phrases

Source of the words: essay-cantonese.txt from rime-cantonese
(https://github.com/rime/rime-cantonese), Debian package rime-data-jyut6ping3.

Copyright: GONG Chen, Ayaka Mikazuki, chaaklau, LeiMaau, laubonghaudoi,
tanxpyox, William915, szc126, Bing Jeung, Henry Chan, graphemecluster,
and other rime-cantonese contributors.
License: CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: scripts/gen_quick_phrases.py keeps multi-character entries inside a
weight floor, drops anything whose characters are missing from the Sucheng
table, and replaces Jyutping with one quick code per word.

Sucheng codes are derived from schemas/kinfx-quick/kinfx_quick.dict.yaml,
itself built from rime-cangjie / rime-quick (LGPL-3.0). The code column is
therefore under LGPL-3.0 as well. See licenses/rime-quick-LGPL-3.0.txt and
the root NOTICE. CC BY 4.0 still applies to the word selection.

  kinfx_hk_words.dict.yaml    — 2-, 3-, and 5-character entries
  kinfx_hk_idioms.dict.yaml   — 4-character band (chengyu and other set phrases)
  kinfx_quick_phrase.dict.yaml — imports the two tables; no preset vocabulary
  kinfx_assoc.dict.yaml       — association suffixes 聯想 (2-4 char words,
                                grouped by first character; scripts/gen_assoc.py)

There is no Cartesian expansion of character readings. A character that has
several quick codes contributes only its highest-weight code.

lexicon/en/extras.tsv adds Gmail and GMAIL (completion targets; Gmail is a
trademark of Google LLC) plus scriptwriting and voiceover. Those rows are
project data. They do not change the SCOWL license of the rest of
kinfx_en.dict.yaml.

lexicon/en/kinfx_names.dict.yaml (v0.5.4) lists brand, product and website
names from Wikidata English labels and aliases, produced by scripts/gen_names.py.
Wikidata data is CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
The names are trademarks of their owners and appear only as typing targets.

lexicon/en/kinfx_en_unsafe.txt is a derived subset (English words Space must
not commit) produced by scripts/gen_english_space.py from the SCOWL list and
essay-cantonese.txt weights. Same SCOWL terms as kinfx_en.dict.yaml; the
selection is project data.

Runtime policy (schemas/lua/melt_eng.lua): prefix completion is raised only
when the input length is at least 4; a length-3 exact dictionary hit may
surface; me, or, and of never do.
